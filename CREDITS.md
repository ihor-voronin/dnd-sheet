## Images

**Character portraits, the site icon (d20), the background hex grid and the wizard's
summoning circle** are our own, drawn in code (`backend/src/dndsheet/visuals/application/portraits.py`,
`scripts/make_icons.py`, `frontend/src/theme.css`). No third-party art in them.

**Emblems** of classes, schools of magic and compendium sections are icons from
[game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) (CC BY 3.0).
We changed only their colour. The site lists them with their authors on `/kb/credits`.
